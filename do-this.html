How the four hours run

Everyone works on their own goal, with the same method. The working floor is the main event, not a side activity.

  1. 00:00The Doing GapWhy knowledge stays unused, and naming the one thing you will do today.
  2. 00:30The USE SessionUnderstand it, simplify it into one realistic action, and agree what “done” looks like.
  3. 01:15The Doing FloorYou work on your real project, with facilitators beside you.
  4. 02:45The Live TestSelected participants show what they made and get real feedback. Everyone submits their work.
  5. 03:30The Next CommitmentEveryone leaves with their next step. Those who want sustained support can apply to the Inner Circle.

Choose your way in

Places are limited, so we select participants who come ready to work. Tell us what you want to do.

We are not asking you to sponsor another motivational conference. We are inviting you to support a programme where people take practical action and produce work you can see.

  • Presenting PartnerSupports the whole event, named with it.
  • Execution PartnerFunds one working area, such as a prototype station or an offer-testing clinic.
  • Support PartnerProvides venue, connectivity, refreshments, equipment or services.

Partners receive agreed visibility, a chance to demonstrate, and an impact report. Attendee contacts are shared only with each attendee’s consent.

A six-week execution cycle for 12 to 15 selected members. You commit to one result, get a deliverable, a check-in and feedback every week, and finish by showing what you actually did.
