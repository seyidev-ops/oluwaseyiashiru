Good questions

Are these the real people?

No. Each advisor is an AI simulation built from that person’s public work: books, letters, talks and interviews. They are not the people and do not speak for them.

How long does a roast take?

About five to ten minutes. Keep the page open while the council works. If you close it by mistake, open the same page again and your roast picks up.

What should I write?

The idea in one line, who it is for, how it makes money, what you have (cash, time, team), your deadline, and which way you are leaning. The more you give, the sharper the roast.

Is it advice?

It is a structured second opinion to help you think. For legal, tax or investment decisions, speak to a professional.
