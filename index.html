    The USE method

    In plain words: I take what you have heard and turn it into steps you can do.

    1. U

      Understand it

      Pick out the few points that matter, then check them. Is it true? Does it work? Is it opinion?

    2. S

      Simplify it

      Put the steps in order, make them fit your life, and shrink the first one to under 30 minutes.

    3. E

      Execute it

      You take the first step and see the result. Then you take the next one.
