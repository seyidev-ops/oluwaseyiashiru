The USE method

In plain words: I take what you have heard and turn it into steps you can do.

  1. Understand it

    Pick out the few points that matter, then check them. Is it true? Does it work? Is it opinion?

  2. Simplify it

    Put the steps in order, make them fit your life, and shrink the first one to under 30 minutes.

  3. Execute it

    You take the first step and see the result. Then you take the next one.

How the system works

  1. You click the icon

    On the home page, everything you've learned is running from you. Clicking the icon stops it.

  2. You answer six short questions

    What you're holding, what it's about, what you want, what stops you, and how much time you have today.

  3. You get your first step

    Your entry is streamlined into three steps and one first to-do, sized to the time you have.

  4. You do it, and tick it
