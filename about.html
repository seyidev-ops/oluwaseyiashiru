A brief introduction

I’m a full stack software engineer, trained in Helsinki (Finland) and with Edureka (India). I program solutions for business growth and development, with 18+ years of combined experience across travel and tours, eCommerce, real estate and religious bodies. I’m certified in cyber security, digital marketing, web development and business development.

I spent my early years in business in digital marketing: training, managing and funnelling sales for the companies I worked with, including Jumia, Konga and AliExpress. Having served over 14 companies and 4 NGOs, I’m committed to helping people break into the remote job space, as employees or freelancers.

That desire led to my current roles as a facilitator at Business Evening School and Lead Facilitator at Everything Remote Job, both subsidiaries of Business Play Limited, which I co-founded, as I did Ona Services Ltd, a business outsourcing company, among others.

I believe in the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, with clarity on the manifestations of eternal life here, as practice for the age to come.

The idea is simple: USE. Understand it, simplify it and execute it.
