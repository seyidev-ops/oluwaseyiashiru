Privacy notice

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to the information you share on oluwaseyiashiru.com. In short: we use it to give you what you asked for (a USE Pack, a roast, a booking or a place at DO THIS) and to follow up with you, and nothing else.

Who is responsible

Oluwaseyi Ashiru, Abuja, Nigeria. Contact: info@oluwaseyiashiru.com or WhatsApp +2347033134979.

What we collect

  • Your answers on the Start page: what you want to put to use, the life area, your description, your goal, what stops you and the time you have.
  • What you share: a link, or a video or audio file you upload.
  • Your contact details: name, email and, if you give it, your WhatsApp number.
  • Payments: when you pay, Paystack handles your card or bank details. We never see them. We receive the payment reference, amount, email and status, and use them to unlock what you paid for.
  • The Roast Room: the idea or decision you describe and the answers you give to the Chair.
  • Speaking requests: your name, organisation, contact details, event details and message.
  • DO THIS: for a place, your name, email, WhatsApp, city, goal, the one thing you want to put to use and what you already have; for partners, your organisation and how you'd like to support; for the Inner Circle, the hours you can give, the result you want, what has stopped you and any link you share.
  • Deeper Systems members: your name, email, plan and payment status.
  • On your device only: your plan, your theme choice and your progress are saved in your own browser so you can come back to them. We can't see these.

How we use it

  • To make your USE Pack and send it to you as a PDF by email.
  • To run your roast in the Roast Room and show you the verdict.
  • To reply to speaking requests, select DO THIS participants, follow up with partners and review Inner Circle applications.
  • To understand where you are and suggest your next step, and to contact you about it.
  • To improve the free and paid systems, using what people find hard (never sold or shared for advertising).

Where it is kept and who processes it

  • Google: your entry is saved in a Google Sheet, and any file in Google Drive, in Oluwaseyi Ashiru's Google account. Emails are sent through Gmail.
  • Google Gemini: your answers and the video you share are sent to Google's Gemini service to read the video and draft your pack. While the site uses Gemini's free tier, Google may use that content to improve its services; on a paid plan it doesn't.
  • Paystack processes payments and keeps your payment details under its own privacy policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude): what you write in the Roast Room is sent to Anthropic's API to run the council. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API content isn't used to train its models by default.
  • Render runs the Roast Room engine (engine.oluwaseyiashiru.com). Roasts are processed there and are not added to our Google Sheet.
  • Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics measure how the site is used (pages visited, device type, rough location). They use cookies, and the data is kept by Google under its own privacy policy. You can block them with your browser's cookie settings or the Google Analytics opt-out.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram): the Meta Pixel records page visits and actions such as sending a form or starting a payment, so we can measure and improve our adverts. Meta keeps this data under its own privacy policy. You can control it in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, or block it with your browser.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website itself and may log basic visit data, such as IP addresses, for security.

These services may store data outside Nigeria. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box before you send your entry.

How long we keep it

We keep entries for as long as we're working with you, and delete them within 24 months of your last contact, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, withdraw your consent, or object to how it's used. Email info@oluwaseyiashiru.com and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to send the entry with you.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes too.
